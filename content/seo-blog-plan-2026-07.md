# TrolinkTek SEO / GEO / AIO Blog Plan

Prepared for: Shenzhen TrolinkTek Technology Co., Ltd.  
Primary audience: automotive electronics importers, distributors, private-label brands, purchasing managers and product managers.

## Topic 1

- Blog title: **CarPlay AI Box vs Wireless CarPlay Adapter: A Distributor’s Product Selection Guide**
- Primary keyword: `CarPlay AI box vs wireless CarPlay adapter`
- Supporting keywords: `CarPlay AI box wholesale`, `wireless CarPlay adapter supplier`, `CarPlay product selection`, `CarPlay AI box buying guide`
- Search intent: Commercial investigation and product comparison.
- Why it is suitable: Buyers often know the two product names but do not understand the different compatibility requirements, user experiences, support burden and price positions. The article can qualify leads before they request samples and naturally links to both product categories.
- Recommended URL: `/blog/carplay-ai-box-vs-wireless-carplay-adapter/`
- Primary CTA: Ask for a product comparison and sample recommendation.

## Topic 2

- Blog title: **Wireless CarPlay Adapter Factory Testing Checklist for Bulk Orders**
- Primary keyword: `wireless CarPlay adapter quality testing`
- Supporting keywords: `CarPlay adapter factory test`, `CarPlay adapter inspection checklist`, `wireless CarPlay adapter bulk order`, `CarPlay adapter quality control`
- Search intent: Supplier evaluation, quality assurance and pre-shipment inspection.
- Why it is suitable: It addresses the risk concerns of procurement teams and demonstrates useful factory knowledge without relying on promotional claims. A structured checklist is also easy for AI search systems to quote and summarize.
- Recommended URL: `/blog/wireless-carplay-adapter-factory-testing-checklist/`
- Primary CTA: Send a vehicle list and request an evaluation plan.

## Topic 3

- Blog title: **Portable CarPlay Screen Buying Guide for Importers and Private-Label Brands**
- Primary keyword: `portable CarPlay screen wholesale`
- Supporting keywords: `portable CarPlay display supplier`, `smart car screen buying guide`, `wireless CarPlay screen OEM`, `portable Android Auto screen`
- Search intent: Product selection, specification comparison and sourcing.
- Why it is suitable: Portable screens serve vehicles without a suitable factory interface and create a separate sourcing opportunity from adapters. Buyers need guidance on display size, mounting, audio routing, camera input, thermal design and packaging.
- Recommended URL: `/blog/portable-carplay-screen-buying-guide/`
- Primary CTA: Request a screen shortlist and OEM/ODM discussion.

## Shared AI-search and SEO implementation

- Open every article with a 40–70 word direct answer to the primary question.
- Use descriptive H2 headings that mirror buyer questions.
- Keep comparison tables as real HTML tables on publication.
- Add `Article`, `BreadcrumbList` and visible-question `FAQPage` structured data only when the FAQ appears on the page.
- Link to the relevant product category, two related blog posts, OEM/ODM page and contact form.
- Use original factory, inspection and application images where available. Label AI-generated images as illustrative internally and never present them as documentary proof.
- Include an author/reviewer line such as “Reviewed by the TrolinkTek Product Engineering Team,” a last-updated date and a short methodology note.
- Avoid unsupported superlatives and absolute compatibility claims. Compatibility must be confirmed against the vehicle, factory head unit, phone and firmware combination.
