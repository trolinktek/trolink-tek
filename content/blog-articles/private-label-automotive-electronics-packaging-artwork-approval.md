# Private-Label Automotive Electronics Packaging Artwork Approval

Private-label packaging artwork should be approved as a controlled product configuration, not as a standalone graphic file. For a CarPlay adapter, AI Box or Smart Car Screen, the carton, insert, label, accessory callout and instruction language can affect SKU identity, customer expectation and support routing. Before production, connect the artwork to the approved product revision, supplied kit, intended market, claims boundary and physical pack-out sample. This prevents a polished carton from describing a different cable, function or compatibility promise than the unit inside.

## Create one approval pack

An approval pack should include the artwork version, product SKU and hardware reference, supplied accessories, barcode or label fields where applicable, market/language scope, insert files and a pack-out sample or clear representation. Identify the owner who approves each part: product, packaging, regulatory or market review as appropriate. Do not add a certification mark, performance statement or compatibility claim unless the responsible team has supplied evidence and authorized wording.

| Approval item | Question | Control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product identity | Does carton identify correct SKU and kit? | Link to controlled BOM and sample |
| Claims | Are feature and compatibility statements bounded? | Product/technical review |
| Accessories | Does image and list match actual pack-out? | Pack-out sample verification |
| Language | Does required market copy match approved source? | Market-language owner |
| Change route | Who approves a later edit or print rerun? | Version and sign-off record |

## Review the physical pack, not only the PDF

Digital approval can miss accessory fit, print placement, protective insert condition or a label applied to the wrong carton. Review a physical sample or controlled proof against the approved configuration. If a supplier substitutes a cable, insert or protective material, treat it as a configuration change. The [kit-configuration error-proofing guide](/blog/automotive-electronics-kit-configuration-error-proofing/) shows why a finished device can pass a functional check while the wrong kit is still packed.

## Scope claims for an uncertain vehicle world

Connected-car packaging often creates avoidable returns when it implies a universal vehicle fit. State prerequisites in a form the customer can act on: a wireless adapter requires working factory wired CarPlay; a portable screen has its own mounting, power and audio decisions; an AI Box should be qualified against its intended host path. Link to a compatibility review or QR-supported support route rather than squeezing an unbounded claim into a carton panel.

## FAQ

### Can a packaging vendor change a file after approval?

Only through the agreed change route. Record the reason, version, owner and affected stock or print run.

### Is a printed sample enough to approve the product pack?

No. Review it with the correct product, accessories and protective materials where practical.

### Who should approve compatibility wording?

The owner responsible for product evidence and customer claims should approve it; avoid relying on a generic marketing phrase.
