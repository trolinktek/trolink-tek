# CarPlay AI Box Thermal Support Triage: What to Record Before Escalation

When a customer reports that a CarPlay AI Box is hot, slow or stops responding, support should record the configuration and observed outcome before naming a cause. Enclosure temperature, placement, vehicle power state, connected host, software activity and ambient conditions can all be relevant. A warm enclosure by itself does not prove a defect, and a support agent should not invent a safe operating threshold or ask a customer to alter vehicle wiring. The practical objective is a reproducible, privacy-aware case record that tells engineering what product state and symptom need review.

## Separate observation from diagnosis

Ask what the user saw: was the device merely warm, did an application slow, did the display disconnect, did audio stop, or did the product restart? Then record product SKU, hardware/firmware identity, vehicle and host path, mounting or storage location, power/start state, connected accessories and the activity immediately before the symptom. Keep subjective heat perception separate from a measured result unless a defined measurement method is used.

| Case field | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Product and firmware | Links symptom to controlled revision |
| Vehicle/host and cable | Separates the connection environment |
| Placement and airflow condition | Identifies installation context without declaring cause |
| Reproduction activity | Gives engineering a focused task |
| Result after normal restart | Distinguishes recovery observation from root cause |

## Use a safe support path

Do not ask a customer to open the enclosure, bypass protective features or place the product in an extreme environment. If an approved instruction says to disconnect a device, follow that controlled route and explain the next step. Where a symptom repeats in a controlled configuration, preserve the evidence and escalate it with the product identity and exact sequence. The [watchdog recovery-testing guide](/blog/wireless-carplay-adapter-watchdog-hang-recovery-testing/) helps separate a recovery observation from a blanket reliability claim.

## FAQ

### Does a hot enclosure prove an AI Box is defective?

No. Record the observed symptom and configuration. A support investigation needs more than a subjective heat report.

### Should an installer move the device without checking the installation plan?

Use the approved installation and service process. Record the original location and any authorized change so the result remains interpretable.

### What should be escalated?

Escalate a reproducible symptom, unexpected restart, loss of intended function or any condition that requires an approved safety or service action.
