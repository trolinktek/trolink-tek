# Automotive Electronics Distributor Co-Marketing Funds: Reimburse Activity, Verify Demand Separately

Automotive electronics distributor co-marketing funds should pay for a defined, approved market-development activity against evidence of delivery and eligible cost. They should not automatically reward supplier orders or treat a campaign invoice as proof of new customer demand. Before approving a Smart Car Screen demonstration, AI Box promotion or installer event, agree on the audience, product configuration, claims, spending boundary, evidence package and reimbursement owner. Then evaluate commercial outcomes in a separate review using reconciled downstream movement and an honest account of measurement limits. For channel directors and private-label importers, this separation makes the decision practical: first establish whether the partner delivered what was funded; then decide whether the result justifies another campaign, a different offer or no further investment.

## Define the funding instrument before discussing a campaign

A co-marketing fund is a shared budget for an agreed promotional activity. Depending on the arrangement, a supplier may reimburse eligible partner expenditure, pay an approved vendor directly or provide specific assets. A market development fund, often shortened to MDF, may serve a similar purpose, but names alone do not establish the terms. Write down what this particular program pays for and how approval works.

A sales rebate is different: eligibility normally depends on an agreed transaction or performance event. A product discount changes the acquisition economics. A sample loan transfers temporary custody of equipment. If an event uses all three, keep each authorization and record separate. Otherwise the same sample, discount or invoice can appear twice in the funding request.

This article addresses approval and closeout of a specific funded campaign. The broader [distributor incentive program guide](https://trolink-tek.com/blog/automotive-electronics-distributor-incentive-program/) addresses which partner behaviors to reward. Funding a demonstration is one possible instrument within that program; it still needs its own scope, evidence and owner. Nothing here establishes that TrolinkTek offers a standing reimbursement program or a standard funding rate.

## Buy a bounded activity with a clear customer question

A useful proposal identifies the decision the audience needs help making. For example, an installer event could help retailers qualify a portable display for older vehicles with different radio inputs. A generic request to increase brand awareness is harder to approve because neither the deliverable nor its usefulness is defined.

State the intended audience, location or channel, dates, exact kit, installation boundary and follow-up route. Record the display, mounting accessories, power supply and approved literature as a configuration, rather than naming only the device family. Specify which campaign assets require review and who can authorize changes if the venue, audience or product changes.

For a CarPlay AI Box campaign, do not borrow a portable-screen demonstration script. Product architecture, receiver prerequisites and application responsibilities differ. Review the proposed assortment against the [Product Center](https://trolink-tek.com/products/) before approving assets. Promotion must follow a supported product offer; it cannot create evidence for compatibility or a feature that has not been qualified.

## Separate authorization, reimbursement and renewal

Treat the campaign as three decisions with different evidence. Preapproval establishes what may be funded. Closeout checks what was delivered and what expenditure is eligible. Renewal decides whether another campaign deserves resources. One signature should not silently approve all three.

| Decision | Evidence to request | Question the owner answers | What it does not prove |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Activity authorization | Audience brief, configuration, deliverables, approved claims and capped budget | Is this a useful and supportable activity to fund? | That the activity will produce incremental sales |
| Reimbursement | Dated delivery evidence, eligible invoices, credits and partner contribution | Was the agreed activity delivered and the claim reconciled? | That every reported sale resulted from the campaign |
| Renewal or expansion | Follow-up outcomes, downstream movement, returns, limitations and lessons | Is the next investment justified by the available evidence? | That results will transfer to another market or product |

Define exception ownership before work begins. An event postponed beyond the agreed window, a substituted kit or a vendor credit requires a recorded decision. Prospective changes are easier to evaluate than a completed activity submitted with a different description. Do not assume an email expressing enthusiasm is spending approval.

## Make the spending boundary inspectable

List eligible cost categories and their basis: a venue booking, approved media placement, local asset production or another specific deliverable. Record whether the basis is an actual eligible expense, a fixed contribution or another agreed method. Define the maximum commitment, currency, submission window, partner contribution and supporting documents without implying a universal percentage.

If a vendor invoice covers several brands or events, agree on an allocation method before authorizing it. Require the claimant to identify credits, cancellations and other funding sources for the same cost. The objective is to reconcile the claim to the approved scope and avoid duplicate funding, rather than collect a large folder of unrelated receipts.

Keep commercial review separate from accounting treatment. The responsible finance team should determine payment documentation, currency handling and local treatment for the arrangement. This workflow does not prescribe tax classification or contract terms. Product purchases, refundable demonstration deposits and promotional expenses should remain visible as separate lines even when they concern one launch.

## Use proof of performance without collecting unnecessary customer data

Proof of performance shows that an approved activity occurred in the agreed form. Suitable evidence might include a dated placement record, approved page version, event agenda, vendor completion record or photographs of the funded setup. Choose evidence that can be matched to the campaign identifier, product configuration and delivery period.

A photograph of an audience does not establish attendance quality, product suitability or sales. An invoice shows a billing request, not necessarily delivery. A campaign dashboard may show attributed actions under its own rules, without establishing whether those customers would have purchased anyway. Record what each item supports and what remains unknown.

Prefer aggregate attendance, redacted records and accountable partner attestations where they meet the review need. Do not make reimbursement depend on uploading personal customer details that the program does not need. Establish access, retention and permitted use through the organization’s applicable process before collecting identifiable lead information. Keep the closeout package useful for verification without turning it into an uncontrolled sales-contact archive.

## A reusable campaign authorization and closeout worksheet

Use one campaign identifier across approval, vendor references, asset versions and the final claim. Maintain version history if scope changes. The worksheet below is an editorial operating template, not an industry certification requirement or a published TrolinkTek commercial policy.

| Worksheet field | Record before authorization | Add at closeout |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customer decision | Target buyer, application question and intended next action | Questions raised and unresolved suitability barriers |
| Product and claims | Sellable kit, evidence references and approved asset versions | Actual kit demonstrated and any approved substitutions |
| Funded deliverable | Channel, location, dates, vendor and measurable completion event | Dated proof matched to that event |
| Spending boundary | Eligible categories, cap, contribution basis, currency and owner | Invoices, credits, allocations and other funding disclosures |
| Follow-up method | Aggregate lead stages, reporting boundary and review window | Qualified inquiries, reconciled movement and incomplete records |
| Decision record | Approval, change route and submission deadline | Reimbursement decision, exceptions and separate renewal decision |

Give each row a responsible person and evidence reference. A worksheet full of unchecked assumptions is a proposal, not a closeout. If a material field is missing, identify the unresolved question and hold the affected decision rather than treating blank cells as zero cost or no exceptions.

## Worked scenario: a portable-screen installer evening

Consider a hypothetical importer planning a Smart Car Screen demonstration with a regional installer partner. Retail staff need to distinguish customers who want visible navigation from those who only need a simpler audio connection. The proposed activity includes one evening session, a defined demonstration kit and an approved product handout. No sales result or customer outcome is assumed.

Before authorization, the importer asks the installer to describe the parked demonstration vehicle, radio input, power route and installation limitations. The handout must state which features are demonstrated in that configuration. The approved expense covers the agreed venue and handout production; a separate record controls custody of the sample. The campaign reference links those records without combining their financial treatment.

At closeout, the venue evidence and final handout match the approval, but the invoice also includes printing for an unrelated accessory brand. The reviewer requests the agreed allocation and checks for a later vendor credit. Reimbursement can proceed for the supported eligible portion according to the arrangement. It need not wait for a claim that the event caused sales if that was never the agreed payment condition.

At the later renewal review, the partner reports several inquiries but cannot consistently distinguish screen requests from general audio-upgrade questions. The importer should improve the intake categories before scaling the campaign. A completed event may deserve reimbursement while still producing insufficient evidence for expansion. That is a useful management conclusion, not a reason to rewrite the original approval after delivery.

## Evaluate demand without overstating attribution

Reconcile campaign-related reports against the same product, reporting period and downstream boundary used in the [distributor sell-through reporting guide](https://trolink-tek.com/blog/automotive-electronics-distributor-sell-through-reporting/). Keep inquiries, quotations, completed sales, cancellations and returns distinct. Supplier shipments and transfers between partner warehouses do not establish end-customer demand.

Incremental impact asks what changed because of the campaign, beyond what would otherwise have happened. [Google’s Conversion Lift documentation](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/12003020?hl=en-GB) describes comparing exposed and unexposed groups for its advertising studies and notes that availability is restricted. That platform-specific method does not validate an installer event or an ordinary before-and-after sales comparison. It illustrates why attributed activity and incremental impact answer different questions.

For a small channel pilot, evidence may support only a directional learning decision. Record stock availability, seasonality, other promotions, partner changes and incomplete reporting. A comparison store can provide context but does not become a causal control simply because it did not host an event. If the design cannot isolate the campaign effect, describe observed movement and uncertainty instead of publishing an uplift claim.

## Decide what to fund next

Renewal should consider the remaining customer barrier, the channel’s ability to deliver and the cost of resolving the next uncertainty. Repeat the activity when evidence supports its purpose; revise it when the audience or follow-up failed; stop when the proposed product or channel cannot support the promise. Do not increase supplier orders merely to make the campaign appear productive.

When a campaign exposes a need for a different kit, localized instructions or revised partner materials, [discuss an OEM/ODM project](https://trolink-tek.com/oem-odm/) before commissioning new promotional claims. Share the target market, proposed assortment, partner responsibilities and the evidence the channel can provide. A controlled offer makes the next promotional brief easier to approve and evaluate.

## FAQ

### Is a co-marketing fund the same as a distributor rebate?

No. A co-marketing arrangement funds specified promotional work or eligible expenditure. A rebate usually depends on a defined transaction or performance event. State the actual eligibility and evidence rules instead of relying on the program name.

### Does an approved invoice prove a campaign generated sales?

No. An invoice supports a cost claim when matched to the agreed delivery evidence and terms. Sales reporting and any incremental-impact assessment require separate evidence, definitions and treatment of uncertainty.

### Should reimbursement always depend on a sales target?

No. Define the payment condition before authorization. If the purpose is to fund a delivered activity, evaluate that obligation separately from renewal. A performance-linked arrangement needs its own agreed recognition event and verification method.

### Can a small pilot claim incremental demand from a before-and-after comparison?

Not from that comparison alone. Stock, timing, other promotions and customer differences may explain the movement. Report observed results and limitations unless the study design and evidence support a causal conclusion.

### What should an importer bring to an OEM/ODM discussion?

Bring the target audience, product configuration, approved claims, campaign deliverables, partner support role and unresolved buyer questions. These inputs help define a supportable kit and asset scope before the channel spends on promotion.
