# TrolinkTek Global B2B Website Strategy

## Positioning

Primary promise: a factory-direct wireless CarPlay and connected-drive OEM/ODM partner for distributors, automotive brands, e-commerce sellers and installers.

Primary conversion: qualified RFQ. Secondary conversions: factory profile request, product consultation, distributor discussion and guide download.

## SEO, AIO and GEO rules

- One intent per page; consolidate spelling variants instead of creating thin duplicate URLs.
- Put manufacturer, supplier, wholesale and OEM pages near the root and link them from product pages.
- Use Product, Organization, BreadcrumbList, Article, FAQPage and VideoObject schema only where matching visible content exists.
- Add concise answer blocks, comparison tables, named authors, update dates, source notes and downloadable specifications for answer-engine citation.
- Publish original factory evidence, testing methods, compatibility methodology and founder/engineer commentary to strengthen entity authority.
- Create English first; add localized subdirectories only when sales can maintain native-quality content and local proof.

## 90-day rollout

1. Days 1–30: launch homepage, core product categories, OEM/ODM, factory, contact, analytics, consent, sitemap and schema.
2. Days 31–60: add distributor/e-commerce/installer pages, compatibility hub, three buying guides and paid-search landing pages.
3. Days 61–90: build comparison content, market pages, case studies, founder video/FAQ content and digital PR links.

## Paid traffic readiness

- Keep campaign landing pages separate from SEO category pages while sharing components and proof.
- Capture source, campaign, product interest, target market, estimated volume and customization need in the CRM.
- Measure form start, form submit, email click, catalog request and qualified-opportunity rate—not only sessions.
